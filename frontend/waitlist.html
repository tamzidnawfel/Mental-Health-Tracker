How Automated Priority Escalation Works for You

When you book a provider whose capacity is currently full, you are automatically enrolled with ROUTINE (0–2 days) priority. As your waiting time increases, the system automatically elevates your position: MODERATE (>2 days) ➔ HIGH (>4 days) ➔ CRITICAL (>6 days), alerting the provider to admit you into their schedule immediately.
